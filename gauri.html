Meet Your Guide

Dr. Gauri Mittal

Physician · Wellness Educator · Author · Singer

A passionate educator of Mind-Body-Lifestyle Medicine, dedicated to helping individuals discover their peak potential through the integration of modern science and ancient wisdom.

Her Story

A Physician Who Lives What She Teaches

Dr. Gauri Mittal is an expert in Lifestyle Disease Management and Mind-Body Medicine. Her journey began at AIIMS Rishikesh where she completed her MBBS, MD, and Post-Doctoral Certificate Course in Lifestyle Disease Management.

Beyond being a passionate educator of Mind-Body-Lifestyle Medicine, she has also written fiction novels published on Amazon, and her work has been recognized in the Amazon Pen to Publish Contest.

One of her novels is about rebuilding a life and finding peace and happiness through purposeful action themes that mirror her approach to wellness education.

Her Philosophy

The Peak Living Approach

The approach of "Peak Living" is based on the teachings of "Soma: Path of the Body" by Dr. Jivasu MD, combined with the latest in lifestyle medicine research.

"Complete personal success is holistic evolution of the individual."

This means addressing every dimension of a person mind, body, identity, and lifestyle with equal depth and scientific rigour.
